# 0004: AGPL-3.0 for code, brand reserved

Status: accepted (2026-10-18)

## Context

The project should stay open and non-commercial in spirit: nobody should turn it into a closed, paid copy.

An open-source licence can't forbid commercial use (that is part of the open-source definition). Licences that do
forbid it, like PolyForm Noncommercial or CC BY-NC, make the project "source-available" instead of open source.

## Decision

- **Code: AGPL-3.0-or-later.** Anyone may run, change and share it, but whoever runs a modified version as a website
  must publish their source under the same licence. That makes a closed commercial clone impossible.
- **Brand: reserved.** The BamDoodle name, logo and visual identity (`brand/`) are not covered by the AGPL. Forks
  are welcome, under their own name and look.
- **Word lists:** our own curated lists under CC BY-SA 4.0, with third-party sources credited in `words/NOTICE`.

## Consequences

Someone could still run an unmodified or open fork commercially, under another name. That's accepted.
