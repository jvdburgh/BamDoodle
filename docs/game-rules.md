# Game rules

The rules as agreed so far. This is a living document: change it in the same PR as the code that changes the
behaviour, and move answered items out of "Open questions".

## The idea

Everyone in the room draws the same secret word together on one canvas, taking turns. Everyone, that is, except
the **bamboozler**: they don't know the word and have to bluff along while the others try to spot them.

## Where it's played

**Phone first.** Everyone plays on their own phone: join with a link or the room code, and the phone shows
everything you need (the canvas, players and mini-maps, timers, your secret, your vote). Tablets and desktops join the
same way and get a roomier layout.

**Optional host screen.** Like a quiz night, whoever creates the room can host it on a TV, beamer or laptop instead
of playing: after the setup, your name and then **Host on this screen** (or **Play on this phone**, "laptop" on a
wide screen with a mouse); the name is needed either way, so the players read who hosts (tapping Host without one
says "Type your name first"). The start page keeps its first screen to Join and Create; who it's for (3 to 8
players, any device, a big screen to host) is in How to play below. The host screen is read from the couch and always fits one screen (a laptop at
1280×800 up to a 1080p TV), with big type throughout. In the lobby it shows the code first, big, beside a QR code of
the room's address ("Scan or go to bamdoodle.io and type BKZW"), the room's settings in one line under Start round,
then who's in as big face tiles. During the round the shared canvas is the stage with everyone's mini-maps beside it,
the header says whose turn it is and the seconds left ("Jip's turn · 20s") under the category, and a footer keeps
"Join at bamdoodle.io BKZW" with a small QR code. In the vote the countdown and "3 of 4 voted" are the biggest things
on screen; the reveal and the word slide in over the drawing's lower part, and the results put the scoreboard (this
round's points and why, with the totals) beside the drawing. It starts the rounds and has the room settings. It never
shows the secret word and doesn't draw or vote. While it's there, no player hosts; a
host screen that reloads takes the role back (its token is on that device), and one gone for 15 seconds hands the
role to the first player. It's a bonus, never required.

**Passing one device round** (a tablet, or taking turns on one laptop) may become a special mode later; it isn't a
goal for now.

## Roles

- **Artists** see the category and the secret word.
- **The bamboozler** sees only the category. They draw along, trying to look like they know the word.
- **Right in your face** as each round starts, a card says it for a few seconds (a second and a half for whoever
  draws first, and it lets taps through since their clock is running; no extra pause before the first turn): "The
  secret word is GIRAFFE. Everyone has this same word, except the bamboozler.", or "You're the bamboozler! Everyone
  else has the word. You only know it's Animals. Draw along and don't get caught!". How to draw it is for the players
  to find out, so the card gives no tips. Clarity wins over a glance across the table. On a phone on its side the card sits over the column
  beside the drawing, not over the drawing. After that the round banner keeps it: the category and the word with
  "everyone's word", or for the bamboozler "???" with "You're the bamboozler" in berry, in the same place and size, so
  the banner's shape doesn't give the role away across the table. The bamboozler's own face wears the fedora (on
  their screen only), and the logo in the corner raises its collar and swirls its hat twice (a quiet static badge with
  reduced motion).
- **"?"** in the room's header opens this room's code and rules (mode, turns, rounds, words) and how to play, any
  time.
- Exactly one bamboozler per round, picked at random by the server. Nobody but the server knows who it is
  until the reveal.

## A game

A game is a few rounds (the host picks how many, default 4). Every round has a new word and a new bamboozler, so a
player's screen counts them as words ("Word 2 of 4"). After
the last round comes the final scoreboard, with confetti for the winner; the host can start a new game from there. The host
can change the number of rounds between rounds; when that's already been reached, the next start begins a new game.

## A round

1. **Lobby.** The start page offers **Join a room** (most people) or **Create a room**, with "How to play" below
   (the steps, the points and a few questions). Joining asks for the four-letter
   room code (on the host's phone: "Join at bamdoodle.io with code …"; on a host screen: "Scan or go to bamdoodle.io
   and type …" beside a QR code) and a nickname, or skips the code when
   you open a shared link (`bamdoodle.io/BKZW`; the address bar shows it once you're in). Creating starts with setting up the
   room ("You can change all of this between rounds."): mode ("How a turn works."), turns each or time per turn,
   rounds per game, the language and the word categories to play with
   (Efteling, Places, Very British, …, and 18+ ones if they want), in two columns on a wide screen (the round and
   the game on the left, the words on the right) and one on a phone: each setting a heading (Mode, Time per turn
   (seconds) or Turns each, Rounds, Language, Categories with how many are picked) with one line on what it does,
   numbers on the choices and a small sketch of what's picked. Under the rounds, a rough length: "About 17 minutes
   with 5 players" (5 players' turns, a line taking about 6 seconds, plus a minute per round for the vote, the guess
   and the results). Back and Next end the form, with the picks in one line beside Next ("Timed · 20 s · 2 turns
   each · 4 rounds · English · 33 categories"). Who it's for (3 to 8 players, any device) is in How to play. Then the host's nickname (after the setup, so it's clearly
   theirs and not the room's), and only then is the room created, with those settings. The host (a player or the
   host screen) can open the settings again between rounds as "Room settings" ("Changes reach everyone right
   away."), back with "Back to the room"; every change there reaches the room at once. Then everyone draws their avatar
   (an empty round pad with one pen and the colours that read on navy; Randomize for a doodle face to start from,
   Clear to start over; left empty, they get a random doodle face; on a phone on its side the pad is on the left and
   the colours and "Go to the room" beside it) and goes to the room. Everyone in the room sees
   each other's faces, also a face finished
   just after a round started. Until a player goes to the room their card says "drawing their face", and the host
   sees "Mo is still drawing their face" next to Start round (they can still start). In the room's lobby, on a phone
   and a laptop alike, the header says how many are in the room and "4 rounds · waiting for Juno to start" ("you
   start when everyone's in" for the host, "Joris starts it from the big screen" in a room hosted by a screen). The
   join card sits on the room's navy and is the one place with the code, with **Share link** for everyone (the
   phone's own share sheet, or `bamdoodle.io/CODE` copied with "Link copied"); the host's Start round is on it too,
   switched off with "2 more players needed to start (up to 8 can play)" while fewer than 3 are here. Under the card
   everyone reads what's picked ("Timed · 20 s · 2 turns each · 4 rounds · English · 12 categories"), with Room
   settings for the host. Under the players, how it goes in three lines: everyone gets the same word, except the
   bamboozler; take turns drawing it on one canvas; vote who was sketchy.
2. **Secret.** The server picks a word from the chosen categories and tells each player their role.
3. **Drawing.** Players take turns on the shared canvas in a shuffled order shown on screen; each player
   gets the same number of turns. The player whose turn it is is highlighted, and their strokes appear live for everyone.
4. **Suspecting, while the drawing goes on.** Any time, a player can point at who they suspect, and change their mind.
   That's public and part of the fun: everyone sees "Noor thinks Jip is sketchy".
5. **Who's sketchy?** After the last turn, everyone makes their final choice: "Who do you think didn't know the word?"
   A tap on a face is your vote, and another tap changes it until the vote ends. Voting ends when everyone has voted
   or after 30 seconds, and anyone who didn't vote votes for whoever they suspected last (or not at all).
6. **Reveal.** The bamboozler and every vote are shown. The bamboozler gets one chance to guess the word, even when
   everyone caught them.
7. **Results.** Who was right, who was wrong, the points of this round and the scores so far. The host starts the
   next round.

## Turn modes

| Mode | A turn is | The host sets |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Timed turns** (default) | free drawing, any number of strokes | seconds per turn: 10, 20 (default), 30 or 60; 2 turns each |
| **One stroke each** | one stroke (press, draw, release), with a 15 second limit | turns per player: 1 to 5 (default 3) |

Two words only: a **round** is one word, one drawing, one bamboozler and one vote; a **turn** is your go with the
pen. In the app the modes are named "Timed turns" ("Draw freely until the timer runs out") and "One stroke each"
("Draw one line, pass it on", with "Turns each: 1 2 3 4 5"); in code and on the wire they're `timed` and
`oneStroke`. The categories are picked as small chips under their word pack's name (Everyday, Culture, Dutch,
World, Film & TV, Trivia, Tech, Logos, 18+), each group with its own Select all or Clear, and Select all (which
leaves 18+ alone) and Clear for everything: a picked one gets a ✓ and a light sun outline, an unpicked one a +, and the
heading counts them: "Categories (12 of 30 selected)". Every non-18+ category of the room's language is ticked by
default, 18+ ones are marked and only in play when ticked.

In one-stroke mode your turn says "Your turn: one line!" (the call-out over the canvas and the banner), and once you
lift the pen the banner says "Line done, passing on…" and the pen stays up until the next turn starts.

The server enforces the turn: strokes from someone whose turn it isn't, or after the time limit, are dropped.
Simultaneous drawing is parked: it is hard to follow who draws what and much heavier to sync.

## Colours and who drew what

Everyone can use every colour: there is nothing to unlock, ever. To still see who drew what:

- **Live highlight:** the active drawer is clearly marked while they draw: a ring around their avatar in the turn
  order, their name in the turn bar, and a name tag that follows their pen tip on the canvas.
- **Mini-maps:** next to each player's name on a laptop, a small canvas shows only *their* strokes. On a phone the
  players are a strip of faces at the top (name, points, the ring on whoever draws, the hat on whoever you suspect, a
  "voted" tag in the vote); a tap opens "See their lines" and "Suspect" ("Suspected ✓" once you do) right under it,
  with who suspects them and, after the reveal, who they voted for and their points. While you can suspect, the faces
  look like something to tap: each but yours shows the fedora faintly (on hover, and always on a touch screen) and
  squishes when pressed, and during the first word, until you suspect someone, a line under the strip says "Tap a face
  to suspect them. Everyone sees it." The mini-maps are back in the final vote.
- **Pen tag:** a small chip with the drawer's avatar and name rides on their pen tip on every screen.
- **Magnifying glass (spotlight):** tap a player's mini-map to highlight their strokes on the main canvas while the
  rest fades back to grey; the sheet and their card get a bold, wobbly hand-drawn frame, and a "Noor's lines · Show
  everyone ✕" chip on the canvas says why and is the way back (so is tapping the mini-map again, or Esc). Hovering a
  player with a mouse previews their spotlight without pinning it. Suspecting or voting never spotlights anyone.
- **Your own card** stands out (an iris outline and a "you" badge), and your face says "That's you" instead of being a
  button. Cards say who suspects whom by name ("suspected by you and Noor"), in berry when you're one of them.

**Suspecting and voting on screen:** on every player card (avatar, name, mini-map of their strokes) a "Suspect"
button under the name, or a tap on the face itself (hovering it shows the logo's fedora faintly), drops the hat onto
their head; the button then says "Suspected ✓" and another tap takes it off. Marking or changing it is public table
talk ("Noor thinks Jip is sketchy", a note over the canvas with Noor's face and Jip's in the hat; "Mo has voted" with
Mo's) and cards say who suspects that player. After the last turn the same button says
"Vote" and the face is the vote too: a tap votes for them (the hat drops on, "Your vote ✓"), a tap on another changes
it. The final vote is a sheet over everything on every screen. Its top stays put while the rest scrolls: "Time's up!
Who's sketchy?", the 30 s countdown, "3 of 6 voted", the secret again since the sheet covers the header (the category
and the word, or "??? You're the bamboozler", the same size), "Who didn't know the word? Tap Vote on them. You can
change it until time's up." and whose suspicion counts if you don't vote. Then the finished drawing, sized to leave
room for the cards, and a small card for every other player: face, name, "voted ✓" beside it once they have (never
for whom), their mini-map as a thumbnail and a quiet Vote button that turns sun with "Your vote ✓". Two columns on a
phone, three when there's room, four on its side or on a laptop; the sheet closes when the vote ends. The room's
header is just the logo, the "?" (with the room code in it during play) and the sound. The round banner says where
the round is: the category and the word (or the bamboozler's "???") on top, then "Noor's turn (1 of 2 each) · 15s",
"Word 1 of 4" at the right and the countdown as a bar across. Rounds are words there, so "Round" never sits next to
"Turn". On a laptop everything fits one screen (the banner in the middle of the header, cards in one column on the
left, the canvas sized to fit with pens and colours right under it). On a phone: the banner full width under the
header, the strip of faces, the canvas, and while someone else draws "Watch closely: who's drawing vaguely? Tap their
face to suspect." under it; on your turn pens and colours are pinned to the bottom, the pen sizes as a small group on
the left and the colours in one row beside them that scrolls and fades out at the right edge. On its side the room
fits the screen without scrolling: the drawing (or, in the lobby, the join card) on the left at the full height, the
header, banner, strip and tools beside it. Then the reveal on the cards themselves: the hat drops
onto the bamboozler, each card says who that player voted for (right or wrong), and once the round is scored each card
pops its points and total. The table talk ("Mo has voted") leaves the drawing as the vote ends. Under the canvas the
headline: who the bamboozler was and what that means for the points ("1 of 3 spotted Sam (+1), so Sam slips through (+1)",
"Everyone spotted Noor! +1 each", "Nobody spotted Sam! Sam +1"), and their guess (the guess's 20 seconds start at the
reveal): "Your one guess at the word (Animals)." and "No worries, we're not too picky about spelling." (true to the
matching under Current defaults). On a phone on its side the bamboozler's field comes first, beside the drawing. Then "Word 1
of 3 was GIRAFFE" (rounds are words on screen, as in the banner), the guess ("Sam guessed it: "giraffe"! +1" or "Sam guessed "horse": not it, no
point."), "This round" (everyone's points with why, popping in, and their totals) and under it the host's "Next round",
or for everyone else who they're waiting for ("Waiting for Juno to start word 2…", or "Joris starts word 2 from the
big screen…"). After the last round
"The End": the podium with everyone's final score and a fun line each from the whole game ("spotted the bamboozler
3×", "slipped through twice", "caught on every bluff"; on a device that saw every round, not after a rejoin) (on a phone when you tap "See the final scores", which every player
has, on the host screen ten seconds after the results), then what's next (the host plays again in the same room, everyone can create
or join another room), our one ask (if you enjoyed it, support Ukrainian citizens) and the footer with who made
it.

## Points (starting point, tune in playtests)

| Who | When | Points |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Artist | final vote for the bamboozler | +1 |
| Bamboozler | guessed the word | +1 |
| Bamboozler | fewer than half of the artists voted for them | +1 |

Split votes need no tie-break: points are per voter, not per majority.

## Words and categories

- English and Dutch, simple and drawable. See [words.md](words.md).
- The host picks which **categories** are in play before starting: everyday ones (Animals, Food, Places, …) and
  themed ones (Efteling, Sinterklaas, Typisch Nederlands, Lekker Nederlands, Very British, Christmas, Fairy tales,
  Landmarks), Logos, media (Films, TV shows, Cartoons and comics, Superheroes, Video games), trivia (Memes, Board
  games, Famous people), Tech, Countries and Cities. A new room starts with every category of its language that isn't 18+, themed ones included; the
  host narrows it down.
- **18+ categories** (innuendo and rude-but-drawable words) are categories like any other, marked 18+ and never in
  play unless the host picks them.

## Current defaults (revisit after playtests)

These are implemented in the server (`server/src/limits.rs`) and easy to change:

- **Players:** 3 to start a round, at most 8 in a room. Designed for 4 to 6.
- **Timers:** one-stroke turns 15 seconds, timed turns as set, the final vote 30 seconds, the bamboozler's guess 20
  seconds. There's no separate round timer: the turns decide how long the drawing lasts.
- **Turn order** is shuffled each round and the bamboozler is picked fully at random, so the same player can be it
  twice in a row (any rule about who it can't be would narrow it down). Words don't repeat until the picked categories run out.
- **Everyone votes, the bamboozler too**, so not voting doesn't give them away; their vote earns nothing. Final votes
  stay secret until the reveal; everyone sees who has voted. Suspicions during the drawing are public.
- **The guess** is free text, forgiving about case, accents, spaces and one typo in words of five letters or more.
  The word may sit inside a longer guess ("Donald Trump" for Trump, "ice bucket challenge" for ice-bucket), but a
  guess that also names, or is, another word of the same category is wrong ("Wit-Rusland" isn't Rusland), so listing
  words doesn't pay.
- **Disconnects:** the round goes on. A drawer who drops loses their turn. A player who drops still counts for the
  vote for 15 seconds (a locked phone shouldn't end it); after that they no longer hold it up. A session token
  takes your seat and score back, and a second device with the same token takes over from the first. After a round,
  the seat of someone who is gone is kept for 2 minutes.
- **Inactive rooms** close after 30 minutes in which nobody joins, draws, votes or changes a setting (an open tab
  alone doesn't keep a room alive); everyone still there is told the room was closed.
- **Late joiners** watch the current round (no word, no vote) and play from the next one.
- **The host** is whoever created the room; when they drop out, the role waits 15 seconds for them (a refresh or a locked phone)
  and then goes to the next player who joined.
